The engagement

Making portal delivery understandable and usable

The Aviation Professionals Club describes itself as a lifestyle and benefits club for eligible aviation professionals in the UAE. Its member services include partner offers, digital membership cards, and access to club information through its website and mobile app.

As Project Manager at Sigma Digital Solutions, I was the primary client contact. I translated requirements into development tasks, shared status updates, supported UAT, and authored a technical user manual covering the portal modules.

Delivery focus

  • Client requirements
  • Actionable development tasks
  • UAT coordination
  • Technical user manual

01 / The challenge

Connecting client needs to shipped work

Requirements clarity

Requests for a member-facing admin portal need to be translated into sufficiently clear tasks that developers can estimate and deliver.

Progress visibility

Regular, understandable updates keep the client informed about work in progress, decisions, and items needing attention.

Knowledge transfer

Portal modules need practical documentation so administrators can take over routine use after delivery.

02 / My contribution

Building clarity from kickoff to adoption

01

Shape development tasks

Translate client requirements into actionable tasks and keep questions moving between the client and delivery team.

02

Share delivery progress

Provide status updates and coordinate validation so expectations stay aligned throughout implementation.

03

Prepare the handover

Document the portal modules in a technical user manual to support a structured transition and ongoing use.

03 / Project impact

A more supported transition into portal use

Module-level documentation and organized handover materials gave the client a clearer reference for using and maintaining the delivered portal. APC describes its wider member offering, including partner benefits and digital membership, on its public site.
