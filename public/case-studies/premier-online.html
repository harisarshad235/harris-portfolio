The engagement

Delivering for a platform with live events

Premier Online describes itself as a provider of online event registration for sports events. Its homepage lists upcoming events in the UAE, Oman, Egypt, Nepal and Saudi Arabia, covering running, triathlon, cycling, swimming, obstacle course races, trekking and more. Visitors can search by sport or country, and the site marks events as sold out, early bird or new date. It also runs in English and Arabic.

I am currently the Project Manager on this platform, responsible for planning, prioritising and coordinating delivery with the product and engineering team.

Delivery focus

  • Delivery planning
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Release coordination
  • Bilingual, multi-country events

01 / The challenge

Changes land while registrations are open

Fixed event dates

Races have a date that cannot move, so registration features have to be ready and stable before sales open.

Many events at once

Dozens of events across several sports and countries are listed at any time, each with its own status such as sold out, early bird or new date.

Two audiences

Organisers need to manage their events, and athletes need a registration flow that is quick to finish, in English or Arabic.

02 / My contribution

Keeping delivery steady

01

Plan around the calendar

Sequence work against upcoming event dates so releases do not collide with peak registration periods.

02

Keep priorities visible

Maintain a clear backlog and regular updates so the team and stakeholders know what ships next and why.

03

Coordinate releases

Work with developers and QA to test changes and release them with minimal disruption to live events.

03 / Project impact

A clearer path from request to release
