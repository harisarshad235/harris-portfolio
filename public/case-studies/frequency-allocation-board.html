Frequency Allocation Board

Digital workflow for spectrum allocation

Replacing paper-based radio spectrum applications with an online portal and a trackable review process.

Government services Application portal Workflow management

The engagement

A digital front door for spectrum services

The Frequency Allocation Board, part of Pakistan's Cabinet Division, manages radio spectrum applications and approvals. The online portal gives applicants a digital way to submit requests and provides a structured route for review and decision-making.

My contribution focused on coordinating tasks and documentation, supporting stakeholder alignment and keeping delivery progress visible across the work.

Portal capabilities

  • Online application submission
  • Automated review routing
  • Application status visibility
  • Radio-frequency service requests

01 / The challenge

Making a manual process easier to navigate

Paper-led applications

Physical submissions and manual handling made the process slower and increased the effort needed to review and route requests.

Unclear progress

Applicants and teams had limited visibility into where requests stood, creating avoidable follow-up and uncertainty.

Many service types

Different spectrum-related services needed a consistent digital process without obscuring their individual review requirements.

02 / The delivery

Digitizing submission and review

01

Online applications

Provide a secure web portal where applicants can submit service requests electronically.

02

Structured routing

Move applications through defined review and approval steps so work reaches the right decision points.

03

More visible progress

Support clearer status tracking and reporting for applicants and the teams responsible for processing requests.

03 / Project impact

A clearer path from request to decision

The portal replaces paper-heavy submissions with a digital application route, helping reduce process friction, improve progress visibility, and support more informed operational decisions.
