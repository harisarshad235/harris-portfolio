Frequency Allocation Board

Public-sector web delivery · Coordination · Documentation

Stakeholders Requirements Documentation Scrumban

Problem

The website effort required strong alignment across stakeholders, ongoing task tracking, and consistent documentation to prevent ambiguity in a public-sector environment. Transparency and coordination were critical for keeping work progressing smoothly.

My role

I supported end-to-end task coordination and documentation, helping maintain visibility across workstreams and supporting a more structured approach to communication and tracking.

Actions

I helped monitor progress, maintain documentation, and support alignment between stakeholders, ensuring the team had a clear view of movement, blockers, and dependencies. This improved accountability and reduced confusion around priorities.

Result

The project benefited from better visibility, cleaner task tracking, and stronger stakeholder alignment. The coordination effort reduced ambiguity and supported steadier execution throughout the website development process.
