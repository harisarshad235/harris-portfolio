Project case study

Taggy Marketplace Platform

Marketplace delivery · Parallel workstreams · Trello coordination

Client coordination Trello Sprint tracking Documentation

Problem

Marketplace delivery ran alongside other software work, making it important to keep tasks, owners, client feedback, and sprint progress visible across parallel workstreams.

My role

As Project Manager at Sigma Digital Solutions, I coordinated Taggy alongside APC and Premier Online, serving as a point of contact for client communication and delivery progress.

Actions

I organized tasks, sprints, and deliverables in Trello, clarified assignments, and kept priorities and deadlines visible to the cross-functional team. I also prepared user manuals to support a clear handover.

Result

The team had a clearer view of concurrent work and upcoming commitments, while structured documentation supported smoother handover and end-user adoption.
