Project case study

CDA Smart Parking Digitization

Digital rollout · Stakeholder alignment · UAT coordination

Agile Delivery RAID UAT

Problem

The program required a coordinated rollout across digital, operational, and stakeholder teams while balancing business needs, implementation constraints, and readiness for launch. The challenge was to keep work moving without losing visibility into dependencies, risks, and release readiness.

My role

I coordinated requirements, sprint execution, and release preparation, helping the team maintain a disciplined approach to delivery and track what still needed to move forward.

Actions

I worked across requirements, risk review, test readiness, and release planning, ensuring that dependencies were monitored and decisions were communicated clearly. This kept the team aligned while supporting a smoother transition from backlog to release.

Result

The program stayed on track through execution and release readiness, with stronger visibility into delivery risks and clearer stakeholder alignment. The effort supported a more controlled digital rollout for a high-visibility public service initiative.
