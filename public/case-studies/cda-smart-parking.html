The engagement

Making city parking easier to manage

This Islamabad parking management system uses automatic plate-number recognition (APNR) and was developed in collaboration with the Capital Development Authority (CDA) to digitize parking operations and improve visibility for drivers and operators.

I coordinated business requirements, sprint execution, UAT, and release. My work included aligning stakeholder expectations and keeping delivery risks and rollout dependencies visible.
