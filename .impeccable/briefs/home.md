# Surface brief: Home (src/pages/index.js) + case studies

Mode: Experience/Persuade hybrid (portfolio). Audience: hirers + clients equally. Actions: View CV, Contact form, Book call.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a transit map of a delivery career. Every section is a station on a coloured line; a route line draws itself down the page as you travel. Refuses the category default of a split hero + uniform card grid + gradient accents.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white poster paper (#F7F7F4) and ink (#0E1116); night service ground #0B0D12. Six flat line colours: cobalt, vermilion, sunflower, green, violet, magenta. Archivo variable, expanded black for display, normal width for text. Thick rounded strokes, 45-degree bends, station dots (white with ink ring), interchange rings, departure-board strips (ink + sunflower). No gradients, no glass.

STORY: Visitor sees the career map converge on Haris, reads the proof ticker, rides the lines through products, client work, skills, credentials, and arrives at the Terminus to contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7/12: giant expanded HARIS / ARSHAD with PMP roundel, title, bio, status and CTAs (View CV primary ink, Download CV, Discuss a project). Right 5/12: map board where six lines named after real career stops converge on the portrait station. Full-width departure-board ticker at the bottom with real metrics.

FORM: Metro Map / Critical Path, user's pick from four candidates (position 1). Seed key: user-pinned (no concept-seed roll; direction chosen via structured question).

Signature interaction: scroll-linked route rail per section that fills in the line colour, with roundel station nodes; header nav is a line map with a sliding train marker. Motion grammar: line-draw (stroke-dashoffset / scaleX) on entry, spring-free ease-out, linear ticker.

Maximal moments: hero map, Products drenched in vermilion, Contact Terminus where all lines converge on ink.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
