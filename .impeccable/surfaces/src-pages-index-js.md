# Surface brief: portfolio home (Experience / Persuade hybrid)

Scope: whole home page and shared chrome. Mode: Experience (the artifact is the career record itself; interface recedes).
Audience: hiring managers/recruiters skimming for scale, proof, credentials; founders/clients looking for a conversation. Actions: open CV, book a call, send the form.
Proof: PKR 700M+ portfolio, PKR 103M+ EVM savings, 8 client case studies, 2 live SaaS products, PMP + PE credentials. Constraints: Next 10 static export, Tailwind 3, light+dark, copy and facts unchanged.
User decision (verbatim): "calm editorial", deep teal / forest accent, full redesign; the previous maximalist transit-poster world is the anti-reference.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A senior delivery leader's briefing document: quiet, ruled, serif-led, where numbers and prose do the persuading. Refuses the loud poster, pills, rails, tickers and card-grid-of-chips.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground (#F6F4EE) over teal-tinted ink (#14201E); one deep teal accent (#0F5E57; #5FC4B3 at night); Source Serif 4 for headings and figures, Hanken Grotesk for text; hairline rules, 6px radii, no shadows at rest; a single deep forest field (#0B3B37) closes the page at Contact and Footer.
STORY: The visitor sees a credible, composed person with scale of delivery proven in numbers, finds the CV or the contact route within seconds, and trusts the discipline because the page itself is orderly and unhurried.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, serif name at display scale with the role line beneath in teal, a 60ch bio, View CV (teal solid) and Discuss a project (text link) with a quiet availability note; right, a 4:5 portrait with a plain caption. Below, a four-row ledger of verified figures separated by hairlines. Header is a hairline-ruled wordmark plus text nav.
FORM: Editorial long-form with two-column section grammar (heading column left, content right), position 1 of the user-pinned direction, seed key: user-pinned (no roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
